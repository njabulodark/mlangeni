Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Introduction

The SK Mlangeni Foundation (“the Foundation”, “we”, “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who interacts with us, including donors, beneficiaries, volunteers, partners, visitors to our website and participants in our events. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have in relation to it.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number and physical address, provided when you donate, volunteer, partner with us or contact us.
  • Donation and sponsorship information: payment details, donation history and chosen sponsorship programmes.
  • Beneficiary information: details of learners, schools and communities participating in our programmes, including academic records where shared with consent.
  • Website usage data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and referral sources, collected through cookies and analytics.
  • Event data: attendance records, photographs and footage taken at Foundation events.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for the purposes for which it was collected, including to:

  • Administer donations, sponsorships, scholarships and programme participation;
  • Provide updates, acknowledgements and impact reports;
  • Manage volunteer, mentorship and partnership relationships;
  • Meet legal, tax and accounting obligations (including Section 18A receipts);
  • Improve our website, programmes and event planning.

4. Legal Basis (POPIA)

We process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), relying on consent, the performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, and our legitimate interests in administering and reporting on our charitable programmes.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with trusted third parties who assist us, such as payment processors, banks, schools, government departments, audit firms and service providers, each bound by confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information where required by law or court order.

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to remember your preferences (such as cookie consent) and, where permitted, to understand how visitors use the site. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the consent banner, or disable cookies in your browser settings.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. We apply reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including access controls and encrypted transfers, to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate or unnecessary information;
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time;
  • Object to processing for direct marketing;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Officer of the Foundation or the Information Regulator of South Africa.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be published on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
